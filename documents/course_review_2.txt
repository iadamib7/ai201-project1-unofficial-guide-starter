Source: Rate My Professors overview
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rate_My_Professors

Notes:
Rate My Professors is a public review website where students can rate professors and campuses. Students commonly use this kind of site when deciding which classes or professors to take. Reviews may discuss professor quality, difficulty, whether students would take the professor again, attendance expectations, textbook use, and short written comments.

Why this matters for the unofficial guide:
This source shows that professor choice is often influenced by informal student reviews rather than only official course catalogs. It is useful background for questions about why students want unofficial course advice.

Possible questions this source can help answer:
- Why do students use professor review websites?
- What kinds of information do students look for before choosing a class?
- What are the limits of relying on anonymous professor reviews?
