Source: Carnegie rule / study workload background
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_rule

Notes:
The Carnegie rule is a common guideline about college workload. It suggests that students may need two or more hours of work outside class for each hour spent in class. This connects to student questions about whether a class feels manageable, time-consuming, or difficult.

Why this matters for the unofficial guide:
Student reviews often mention workload, time commitment, and whether a class takes more time than expected. This source gives background for why workload is an important part of course planning.

Possible questions this source can help answer:
- Why do students care about workload before choosing a class?
- How much outside study time might a course require?
- Why might a class feel harder than the official description suggests?
