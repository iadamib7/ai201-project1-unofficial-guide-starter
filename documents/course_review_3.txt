Source: Critical Review at Brown University
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_Review_%28Brown_University%29

Notes:
The Critical Review is a student-run publication at Brown University that reviews course offerings. Reviews are written from student course evaluation questionnaires. The purpose is to help students make informed course choices and to give instructors feedback based on student experiences.

Why this matters for the unofficial guide:
This source is directly related to the project idea because it shows an example of students organizing course knowledge for other students. It also shows that course reviews can supplement official course descriptions with real student experiences.

Possible questions this source can help answer:
- Why are student course reviews useful?
- How can student experiences help future students choose courses?
- What is missing from official course listings?
