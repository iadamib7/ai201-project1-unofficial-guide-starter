Source: Student SPILL peer support overview
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student_SPILL

Notes:
Student SPILL was a peer-to-peer online support system for college students. Students could anonymously share problems, and trained student volunteers would send empathetic responses and suggest resources. The system was designed around peer support and student comfort.

Why this matters for the unofficial guide:
This source shows that students often need informal peer support, not just official information. It is useful for questions about why students may prefer anonymous or student-centered resources when they feel stressed or uncertain.
