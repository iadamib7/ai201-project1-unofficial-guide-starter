Source: Writing center overview
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writing_center

Notes:
Writing centers help students with papers, projects, reports, and other writing assignments. They commonly offer one-on-one tutoring, workshops, appointments, and drop-in help. Writing centers are usually meant to support learning and revision rather than simply editing or grading a student paper.

Why this matters for the unofficial guide:
Student advice often points classmates toward helpful campus resources. This source is useful for questions about what support resources students can use when they are struggling with writing-heavy assignments.
