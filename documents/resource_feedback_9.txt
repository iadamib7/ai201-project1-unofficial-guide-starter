Source: Ask Foy at Auburn University
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ask_Foy

Notes:
Ask Foy is an information service at Auburn University. It was originally designed as a resource for Auburn students looking for course information, grades, or campus services. Student operators help answer questions from callers or visitors.

Why this matters for the unofficial guide:
This source shows that students often need help finding practical information about campus life and courses. A searchable guide can serve a similar purpose by helping students locate answers quickly.
