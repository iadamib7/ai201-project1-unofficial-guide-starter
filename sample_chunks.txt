∩╗┐Source: Rate My Professors overview
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rate_My_Professors Notes:
Rate My Professors is a public review website where students can rate professors and campuses. Students commonly use this kind of site when deciding which classes or professors to take. Reviews may discuss professor quality, difficulty, whether students would take the professor again, attendance expectations, textbook use, and short written comments. Why this matters for the unofficial guide:
This source shows that professor choice is often influenced by informal student reviews rather than only official course catalogs. It is useful background for questions about why students want unofficial course advice.

∩╗┐Source: Critical Review at Brown University
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_Review_%28Brown_University%29 Notes:
The Critical Review is a student-run publication at Brown University that reviews course offerings. Reviews are written from student course evaluation questionnaires. The purpose is to help students make informed course choices and to give instructors feedback based on student experiences. Why this matters for the unofficial guide:
This source is directly related to the project idea because it shows an example of students organizing course knowledge for other students. It also shows that course reviews can supplement official course descriptions with real student experiences.
